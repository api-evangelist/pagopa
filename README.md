PagoPA S.p.A. is the Italian state-owned company (owned by the State through the Ministry of Economy and Finance, supervised by the Presidency of the Council of Ministers) whose mission is to design and build the State's digital infrastructure for public digital services. Its products include the pagoPA payments platform, the IO public services app, SEND (digital notifications), the National Digital Data Platform (PDND Interoperability), PARI, Check IBAN and the European Digital Identity Wallet, with APIs and guides published on its Developer Portal (developer.pagopa.it) and docs.pagopa.it.

**Areas:** Company · Payments · Government · Public Sector · Digital Identity · Interoperability · Notification · Italy

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for PagoPA: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
